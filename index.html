Quads · Hamstrings · Glutes · Bike · ~55 min + cardio
Lower Body — Knee Safe
QUAD
Leg Press (Machine) 🏆
Keep knees at 90° max. Safer than squats. Controlled range of motion.
QUAD
Leg Extension 🏆
2-second squeeze at peak. Isolated quad work. No impact on knees.
HAM
Leg Curl 🏆
Slow negative. 3-second descent. Hamstring development supports lower body.
GLUTE
Glute Bridge 🏆
1-second squeeze at top. Glute activation. Can use bodyweight or light weight.

CARDIO & RECOVERY
Active recovery · Zone 2 pace · Steady state
🚴

STEADY CARDIO

Rowing machine, stationary bike, or easy walk. 20–30 minutes at conversational pace. Zone 2 fat-burning. Log your duration and modality. This is where waist reduction happens.

CARDIO
Steady State Session 🏆

Full rest is best. If you move, keep it easy: 20–30 min walk, gentle yoga, or stretching. No training intensity. Recovery is when adaptation happens.
